Our Story

How It Started

From a weekend hobby to Jaipur's favourite cookie jar

Cookie Wookie started in a small home kitchen with one mixing bowl, an old family oven, and cookies made for friends who kept asking for "just one more batch." Word travelled fast, and what began as a weekend hobby slowly turned into a proper little bakery.

Every recipe here is tested, tasted and re-tested until it's right — thick, chewy cookies with real butter and good chocolate, and mini cakes soft enough to make any small celebration feel special. Nothing leaves the kitchen unless it's something we'd happily serve our own family.

Our Journey

Milestones, so far

Year One

First batch, first customers

Started baking cookies for family and friends out of a home kitchen — and quickly ran out of hands to package orders.

Year Two

Mini cakes join the menu

Added celebration mini cakes after one too many birthday requests, and built out proper packaging for delivery.

Year Three

Gift boxes & festive hampers

Launched curated gifting boxes for festivals and corporate gifting across Jaipur.

Today

500+ happy orders and counting

Still small-batch, still handmade — just with a lot more practice (and a much bigger oven).
