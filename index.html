Our Story

A home kitchen, an old family oven, and a very stubborn love for butter.

Cookie Wookie began as weekend baking for friends and grew into Jaipur, Rajasthan's go-to for warm, chunky cookies and mini celebration cakes — every batch mixed, baked and boxed by hand, no preservatives, no shortcuts.
